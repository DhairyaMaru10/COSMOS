A world of its own

Meet Earth

Earth is the only world known to host life. Liquid water covers about 71 percent of its surface, and a nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere moderates temperatures and shields the surface from much harmful radiation.

Moving tectonic plates build mountains, open ocean basins, and recycle crust. A liquid iron-rich outer core helps generate Earth’s magnetic field. Air, water, rock, and life shape a constantly changing planet.

The biosphere extends from deep oceans to the atmosphere. Earth-observing satellites measure global change, while the International Space Station provides a laboratory for studying the planet and living in microgravity.

Moons of Earth.

Moons and companions

The Moon is about 384,400 km away, raises tides, and helps stabilize Earth’s axial wobble. It is slowly receding by about 3.8 cm per year.
